# Recorded sound credits

All distributed recordings are CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
The manifest gives every output hash, source URL/hash, exact edit spans and processing. Freesound HQ previews are lossy publisher derivatives, not original WAV files.

No Kenney assets or generated sound are distributed. Grass and sand share recorded sand contacts; all construction materials share recorded wood-block contacts. Underwater is a filtered perspective of the coastal recording, not a hydrophone capture.
