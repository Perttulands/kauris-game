Make a home

Grown-up controls & help

Dig, choose a seed, cover it, then hold the watering can. Keep the soil moist. Hold the axe on a mature tree to chop; flowers gather gently. Wild trees around the island also give materials.

Build floors first, walls on floors, roofs above walls. Q/E or wheel chooses; M changes material, R switches wall axes (aim near either edge), Z/X changes level. Remove from the top down for a full refund.

WASD walks, Shift runs, Space jumps. If mouse capture is unavailable, use arrow keys or right-drag to look. Space rises in water; C or Ctrl dives. Follow the coral picture to the reef and build on the wide sandy clearing. Enclosed homes with a door and roof welcome diver neighbours too. Click the paused background to play again. Escape pauses. Seeds and water are unlimited.
